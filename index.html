Ramesh Sharma, Owner

A kadhai my father lit in 1987

My father, Late Shri Gopal Sharma, started this shop with one kadhai and one helper in 1987. I still remember sitting on a jute sack at age eight, watching him stir the first batch of motichoor.

My uncle Mohan taught me how to judge ghee by smell alone. Today my son Ankit helps at the counter, but the recipes have not changed, and I still taste every batch myself.
